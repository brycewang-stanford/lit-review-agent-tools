## MinerU

[github.com/opendatalab/MinerU](https://github.com/opendatalab/MinerU) · 📄 PDF → Structured Data Extraction · ~75.8k★ · 🟢 · `Apache-2.0`

**Why this one.** The most accurate PDF→Markdown converter for scientific documents: formulas, tables and 100+ languages, all local.

**When not this one.** Heavy. If you want something lighter for straightforward papers, docling converted a 29-page paper in 78s on CPU (recipes/04) with tables intact; marker is faster still.

## academic-research-skills

[github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills](https://github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills) · 🌟 All-in-one Research Agents & Skills · ~39.7k★ · 🟢 · `CC-BY-NC-4.0`

**Why this one.** The most complete research→paper pipeline for Claude Code, and the only pick with citation integrity gates that cross-check three sources.

**When not this one.** Its CC-BY-NC-4.0 licence forbids commercial use — a real constraint the star count hides. It also assumes you work inside Claude Code; there is no library to import.

## STORM

[github.com/stanford-oval/storm](https://github.com/stanford-oval/storm) · 🔎 Deep Research & Auto Survey Generation · ~30.3k★ · 🟡 · `MIT`

**Why this one.** Best-in-class for turning a topic into a cited, Wikipedia-style long article, with published research behind the two-stage approach.

**When not this one.** It writes a *survey*, not a systematic review — there is no screening, no PRISMA trail, no reproducible inclusion criteria. Needs an LLM key plus a search backend, and has not been pushed to in over three months.

## gpt-researcher

[github.com/assafelovic/gpt-researcher](https://github.com/assafelovic/gpt-researcher) · 🔎 Deep Research & Auto Survey Generation · ~28.7k★ · 🟢 · `Apache-2.0`

**Why this one.** The most reliable general-purpose 'go research this' agent, and the least academic-specific — useful when your question spans grey literature and the web.

**When not this one.** Web-first, so coverage of paywalled and indexed scholarly databases is weaker than a dedicated OpenAlex/PubMed client. Requires both an LLM key and a search API.

## AI-Scientist

[github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist](https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist) · 🧪 Autonomous Science: idea → paper · ~14.3k★ · 🟡 · `custom`

**Why this one.** The most ambitious system in the catalogue and the best one to *read* if you want to see how far end-to-end automation currently goes.

**When not this one.** Not a literature-review tool: it runs experiments and writes papers, at real API cost, under a bespoke non-standard licence. Output is workshop-grade. Treat as a research artefact, not infrastructure.

## paper-qa

[github.com/Future-House/paper-qa](https://github.com/Future-House/paper-qa) · 📚 Paper Q&A and RAG · ~8.9k★ · 🟢 · `Apache-2.0`

**Why this one.** The highest-accuracy way to ask questions across your own PDFs and get answers that carry citations back to the source passage.

**When not this one.** Needs an LLM key, and cost scales with corpus size. It answers questions over papers you already have — it will not find them or screen them for you.

## zotero-gpt

[github.com/MuiseDestiny/zotero-gpt](https://github.com/MuiseDestiny/zotero-gpt) · 🗂️ Reference & Knowledge Management (Zotero / Obsidian) · ~7.3k★ · 🟢 · `AGPL-3.0`

**Why this one.** Chat with your library inside Zotero itself, with no second application to run.

**When not this one.** Interactive by design — it is a reading aid, not something you can put in a pipeline. AGPL-3.0, which matters if you plan to redistribute.

## zotero-mcp

[github.com/54yyyu/zotero-mcp](https://github.com/54yyyu/zotero-mcp) · 🔌 MCP Servers · ~4.4k★ · 🟢 · `MIT`

**Why this one.** The most complete bridge between a Zotero library and an AI client: semantic search, PDF full text and citation analysis over what you have already collected.

**When not this one.** Useless unless you already keep your library in Zotero and use an MCP-capable client.

## arxiv-mcp-server

[github.com/blazickjp/arxiv-mcp-server](https://github.com/blazickjp/arxiv-mcp-server) · 🔌 MCP Servers · ~3.0k★ · 🟢 · `Apache-2.0`

**Why this one.** Search arXiv and pull papers into context as Markdown without leaving your assistant; ships a one-click `.mcpb` bundle.

**When not this one.** arXiv-only. For biomedical work PubMed-MCP-Server or paper-search-mcp will cover far more of your field.

## ASReview

[github.com/asreview/asreview](https://github.com/asreview/asreview) · 🧮 Systematic Review & Screening · 956★ · 🟢 · `Apache-2.0`

**Why this one.** The only pick here with a measured claim: on a published review's own data it surfaced all 38 relevant papers after screening 5.5% of 4,544 records (see recipes/02). Academic provenance, runs fully offline, no key.

**When not this one.** It ranks; you still decide. There is no automatic include/exclude, and no way to see your recall mid-review, so you need a stopping rule. Least-starred pick in the catalogue — popularity is not the signal here.
